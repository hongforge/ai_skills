# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn and collaborate. Be
respectful, assume good faith, and focus critique on content and ideas.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers
may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the
community.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer. If no private contact is
available, open a minimal issue requesting maintainer contact without naming
people or including personal information.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
project spaces. Maintainers may warn participants, remove content, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban accounts depending on severity and
repeated behavior. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and
applied consistently.
