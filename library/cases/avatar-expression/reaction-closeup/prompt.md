# 情绪反应特写

## Prompt

Create one close-up reaction avatar of {{character_description}} experiencing {{emotion}}. Frame the face and upper shoulders so eyes, eyebrows, and mouth can communicate the emotion clearly even at small size. Use {{visual_style}} with consistent facial proportions, natural eye alignment, a simple {{background_tone}} background, and soft directional lighting that supports the expression. Keep the emotion specific and believable rather than exaggerated; preserve the same hairstyle, clothing anchors, and camera angle for future variants. No full body, no multiple expressions in one image, no speech bubble, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## 使用说明

适合剧情反应卡、社媒回复和角色状态图；一次只指定一种情绪，例如克制惊讶、无奈、欣慰或警觉。
