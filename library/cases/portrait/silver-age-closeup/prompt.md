## Prompt

Create a respectful, photorealistic close-up portrait of {{subject_description}} in {{setting}}. Use a head-and-shoulders composition only, with the face filling around 70% of the frame and both eyes in crisp focus. The subject wears {{wardrobe}}. Illuminate with soft directional daylight and a gentle warm bounce, creating dimensional cheekbones and a calm, direct expression. No full body, no distant framing, no hands or objects in front of the face, no glamour retouching, no exaggerated wrinkles, no warped facial features, no text, no logo, no watermark.
