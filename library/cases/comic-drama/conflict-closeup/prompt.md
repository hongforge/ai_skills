# 漫剧情绪冲突特写

## Prompt

Create a vertical 9:16 comic-drama confrontation close-up. Show {{protagonist}} in sharp near-portrait focus reacting to {{conflict}} with {{opposing_character}} implied through a blurred shoulder, reflected silhouette, or controlled off-camera eyeline. Set the moment in {{setting}} and shape it with {{lighting}}. The face and eyes must carry the scene; preserve the protagonist's established hairstyle, costume anchors, and facial construction. Use restrained cinematic color separation, shallow depth, one intentional prop or background clue, and a clear lower-third area for optional post-production copy. No full body, no violence graphic detail, no speech bubbles, no excessive tears or effects, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## 使用说明

适合误会、对峙、秘密揭露等情绪高点；对手不必完整露面，以便把观众注意力锁在主角反应上。
