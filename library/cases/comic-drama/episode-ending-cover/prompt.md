# 漫剧章节结尾封面

## Prompt

Create a polished vertical 9:16 comic-drama episode-ending cover. Feature {{protagonist}} in {{setting}} at the quiet moment after a major event. Express {{unresolved_question}} through one subtle {{relationship_clue}} in the composition, not through written exposition. Keep the protagonist's face or silhouette as the primary emotional anchor, use {{palette}} with a controlled light falloff, and preserve series-consistent hairstyle, wardrobe, props, and world details. Create enough clean negative space for a future episode number, while delivering a complete image without any actual text. No collage, no full credits layout, no speech bubbles, no platform interface, no logo, no watermark.

## 使用说明

适合章节收尾、下一集预告和连载封面；悬念应通过一个关系或道具线索表达，而不是堆叠多个剧情事件。
