# 漫剧剧情转折场景

## Prompt

Create one vertical 9:16 comic-drama turning-point scene in {{location}}. Show {{protagonist}} performing {{decisive_action}} as {{clue}} becomes visible. Establish the action through foreground, middle distance, and a simple background plane; every object must support the causal event. Use {{visual_style}} with one directional light source, a controlled color accent for the clue, stable character costume anchors, and a strong vertical composition that guides the eye from clue to reaction. Keep the world physically believable and leave a clean upper or lower zone for optional editorial treatment. No multi-panel layout, no unrelated crowd, no impossible perspective, no speech bubbles, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## 使用说明

适合身份反转、证据出现和人物作出决定的节点；线索只保留一个，避免把关键帧画成复杂线索墙。
