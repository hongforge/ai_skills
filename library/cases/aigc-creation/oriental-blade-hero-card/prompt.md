# AIGC 东方女剑客角色卡

## Prompt

Create one vertical character-card key visual for an original {{character_archetype}}. Build her identity around {{appearance_anchors}} and the repeatable silhouette of {{signature_weapon}}. Use a three-quarter portrait with a crisp, expressive face, natural anatomy, layered costume construction, and restrained motion in hair or fabric. Let {{faction_palette}} guide the armor accents, atmospheric backdrop, and one controlled rim light; render in {{render_style}}. Reserve a clean title-safe zone while keeping the image readable as a small card. No alternate costumes, no extra characters, no existing IP, no readable text, no logo, no watermark.

## 使用说明

适合东方幻想游戏的招募页、角色档案和系列卡面。后续角色需要固定脸部识别点、材质规则、武器比例与阵营主辅色。
